# Medical Records Handling SOP — China Hospitals Guide

Standardize how inbound patient medical records are received, processed, and turned into hospital inquiries. Applies to every case review (L1 / L2 / L3).

## 1. Intake

Collect what is available, and record what is missing:

- Diagnosis / suspected diagnosis
- Treatment recommendation already given by their doctor
- Imaging (MRI / CT / PET / ultrasound) — reports and files
- Pathology reports
- Discharge summaries
- Current medications
- Prior surgeries / treatments
- Country, age, language preference, travel window, budget

Flag anything unreadable or incomplete to the patient within 24 hours.

## 2. Processing workflow

1. **Receive** — confirm receipt to the patient; log into the case tracker.
2. **Organize** — one folder per case; files named by type (`diagnosis / imaging / pathology / discharge`).
3. **Translate** — key records into Chinese (for hospitals) and English (for patient-facing comparison). Never send machine translation without human review.
4. **Summarize** — one-page case summary using the hospital-inquiry template.
5. **Prepare inquiry** — fill one template per hospital.
6. **Send to hospitals** — max 2–3 hospitals; log send date and contact person.
7. **Receive responses** — log each hospital's reply.
8. **Translate responses** — back into the patient's language.
9. **Compare** — side-by-side: acceptance, timing, cost estimate, English support.
10. **Patient decision** — present options with a recommendation; hand over the hospital contact.

## 3. Human review (mandatory)

AI may organize, translate, or summarize — but every diagnosis, treatment, hospital recommendation, and cost figure is reviewed by a human coordinator **before** it reaches the patient or a hospital. No AI output is sent unedited.

## 4. Privacy

- Records are confidential medical data. Store them in the case tracker; do not share outside the patient's engagement.
- Do not attach sensitive files to public web forms — patients are directed to send records via WhatsApp / Telegram / email.
- Archive or delete per retention policy after case closure.

## 5. Response-time targets

- Confirm receipt: within 4 business hours
- Case summary + first hospital inquiry: within 1–2 business days
- L2 Hospital Verification delivered: 4–7 business days (as advertised)
