## Quick Answer: Heart Bypass Surgery in China, 2026

**If you need coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG), expect $15,000-$30,000 in China** — about 40-50% below Singapore, 50-70% below the US, with a **2-4 week wait** after pre-evaluation instead of months-long queues. The three hospitals international patients choose most are **Fuwai (Beijing) for volume and complex cases, Anzhen (Beijing) for aortic disease, and Zhongshan (Shanghai) for minimally invasive options**. All three have English-speaking international departments, accept remote case review, and issue visa invitation letters. The full process — from sending records to discharge — typically takes 2-3 weeks in China.

## 1. Heart Bypass Surgery Cost in China vs Singapore, Thailand & US (2026)

| Procedure | China (USD) | Singapore (USD) | Thailand (USD) | USA (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coronary Bypass (CABG) | $15,000 – $30,000 | $30,000 – $60,000 | $25,000 – $45,000 | $100,000 – $200,000 |
| Heart Valve Replacement | $12,000 – $20,000 | $25,000 – $50,000 | $18,000 – $35,000 | $80,000 – $150,000 |
| TAVR (Transcatheter Valve) | $18,000 – $30,000 | $40,000 – $70,000 | $25,000 – $45,000 | $150,000 – $300,000 |
| Aortic Surgery | $25,000 – $40,000 | $45,000 – $80,000 | $30,000 – $55,000 | $150,000 – $300,000 |
| Congenital Heart Repair | $10,000 – $20,000 | $20,000 – $40,000 | $15,000 – $30,000 | $80,000 – $150,000 |

> 2026 estimates based on published hospital pricing, patient-reported quotes, and our direct hospital visits. Final cost depends on your specific case — always request a written itemized quote from the international department. Travel, accommodation, and 7-14 days of pre-op tests are not included in these figures.

**What's included in a typical quote:** surgeon and anesthesia fees, ICU and ward stay, the bypass grafts / valve prosthesis, standard medications, and English-language discharge records. Most hospitals ask for a 30-50% deposit to lock the surgery slot; the remainder is payable before discharge.

| Phase | What Happens | Duration |

| **3. Visa & booking** | Hospital issues medical visa invitation letter (S2 visa); book flights | 1-2 weeks |

| **5. Surgery** | CABG typically 3-6 hours; ICU recovery 1-3 days | 3-6 hours + ICU |
| **6. Recovery & discharge** | Total hospital stay 5-10 days; complete English records + follow-up plan | 5-10 days |

## 6. Is Heart Surgery in China Right for You?

**Consider it if:**
- You're uninsured or underinsured, and your local quote is financially devastating
- You need a complex procedure and want a high-volume specialist center
- Your local waitlist is months long and your condition allows travel
- You can spend 2-3 weeks in China (surgery + recovery)

**Think twice if:**
- Your insurance fully covers treatment at home
- Your condition makes travel unsafe (ask your cardiologist)
- You need emergency surgery today — travel time is a factor

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does heart bypass surgery cost in China in 2026?
CABG costs **$15,000-$30,000** at top Chinese cardiac centers, vs $30,000-$60,000 in Singapore, $25,000-$45,000 in Thailand, and $100,000-$200,000 in the US. Valve replacement: $12,000-$20,000. TAVR: $18,000-$30,000. (2026 estimates based on published data; final cost depends on hospital and case complexity.)

### What is the #1 heart hospital in China?
**Fuwai Hospital (Beijing)** — consistently ranked China's #1 cardiac center, with the highest surgery volume in Asia. Anzhen leads in aortic surgery; Zhongshan (Shanghai) leads in minimally invasive techniques.

### How long is the wait for heart surgery in China?
**2-4 weeks** for elective CABG after pre-evaluation, vs 3-6 months in many public systems. Emergency cases are handled immediately. Add 7-14 days of pre-op workup after arrival.

### Is cardiac surgery in China safe?
Top centers report **CABG operative mortality around 1%** (vs 2-3% international standard), with surgeons performing 200+ procedures per year. Fuwai, Anzhen, and Zhongshan are JCI-accredited or top-tier 3A hospitals with English-speaking international departments and full ICU support.

### Can I get minimally invasive or robotic heart surgery in China?
Yes. Zhongshan specializes in keyhole bypass and valve surgery; Anzhen uses Da Vinci robots. TAVR is available at Fuwai, Anzhen, and West China Hospital — all with 500+ procedures of experience.
