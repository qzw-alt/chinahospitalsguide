## Quick Answer: Lung Cancer Treatment in China, 2026

**Lung cancer treatment in China costs $15,000-$30,000 for surgery (lobectomy) and $30,000-$60,000 for a full treatment course including chemo or immunotherapy** — 75-80% below US pricing, with Chinese PD-1 immunotherapy at $10,000-$18,000 per year versus ~$150,000 in the US. **CAMS Cancer Hospital (Beijing)** is China's #1 lung cancer center with 18,000+ lung surgeries per year; Shanghai Chest Hospital, Fudan University Shanghai Cancer Center, and Peking University Cancer Hospital are the other leading options. Most international patients start treatment **2-4 weeks after their records are reviewed**, and a full surgical episode in China takes 3-4 weeks including recovery.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does lung cancer treatment cost in China in 2026?
Lobectomy costs **$15,000-$30,000**; a full course including chemo or immunotherapy runs **$30,000-$60,000**. Chinese PD-1 immunotherapy is $10,000-$18,000/year vs ~$150,000/year in the US. (2026 estimates based on published data; final cost depends on stage and plan.)

### Which hospitals in China are best for lung cancer treatment?
**CAMS Cancer Hospital (Beijing)**, **Shanghai Chest Hospital**, **Fudan University Shanghai Cancer Center**, and **Peking University Cancer Hospital** lead for international lung cancer patients. CAMS has the highest surgical volume (18,000+ lung surgeries/year); Fudan and Peking University Cancer Hospital have the strongest systemic therapy and trial programs.

### How long does lung cancer treatment take in China?
Surgery requires a **3-4 week China stay**; radiation or immunotherapy courses run 4-8 weeks. Records review takes 48 hours, and a written plan and quote arrive within 1-2 weeks before you travel.

### Can I access immunotherapy and targeted therapy in China?
Yes — China has several domestically developed PD-1 inhibitors (sintilimab, toripalimab, camrelizumab) at 75-90% below US prices, plus EGFR TKIs and 140-250+ active clinical trials at the major cancer centers.

### What do I need to send to start the process?
The latest **pathology report, CT/PET imaging, prior treatment records, and molecular test results (EGFR, ALK, PD-L1)**. The hospital reviews them in about 48 hours and issues a written treatment plan and cost estimate before you book travel.
