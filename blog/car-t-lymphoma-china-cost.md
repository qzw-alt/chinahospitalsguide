## Quick Answer: CAR-T for Lymphoma in China, 2026

**CAR-T therapy for relapsed/refractory lymphoma in China costs $89,000-$151,000 (commercial) or $30,000-$80,000 (clinical trial)** — 60-85% below US pricing and roughly 40-60% below Singapore, with **no waitlist for eligible patients** (most slots book 2-4 weeks after eligibility is confirmed). China has more NMPA-approved CAR-T products than the US or EU, and CD19 CAR-T for DLBCL — the most common lymphoma — is available at multiple top-tier hematology centers with English-speaking international departments. The full journey, from records review to infusion, takes about 4-6 weeks in China.

## 1. How Much Does CAR-T for Lymphoma Cost in China? (2026)

CAR-T pricing in China has **two tiers**, which is why quotes for the same therapy can differ by 3-5x between patients.

| Access Route | 2026 Price (USD) | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial (NMPA-approved CD19 CAR-T)** | $89K – $151K | Patients who want an approved product with no trial eligibility requirements |
| **Clinical trial / sponsored access** | $30K – $80K | Eligible patients willing to enroll in a hospital- or manufacturer-sponsored trial |

### China vs Singapore vs India vs US (CD19 CAR-T for Lymphoma)

| Country | Commercial Price (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **China** | $89K – $151K | Carteyva (relmacabtagene) and Yescarta-type CD19 products; 7 NMPA-approved CAR-T products total |

| **India** | Not approved commercially | Trial access only; limited slots |

> 2026 estimates based on published pricing. China's cost advantage comes from domestic manufacturing (Carteyva is China-developed) and lower hospitalization costs. Travel and accommodation are not included in either figure.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does CAR-T therapy for lymphoma cost in China in 2026?
Commercial CD19 CAR-T for lymphoma costs **$89,000-$151,000** in China. Clinical trial access can reduce this to **$30,000-$80,000** for eligible patients. The same therapy costs $300,000-$500,000 in the US and $280,000-$350,000 in Singapore. (2026 estimates based on published data; final cost depends on hospital and case complexity.)

### Which lymphoma patients are eligible for CAR-T in China?
Most programs target **relapsed or refractory DLBCL** after at least two prior lines of therapy, plus other CD19-positive subtypes (follicular lymphoma, mantle cell lymphoma). Requirements include CD19 expression, adequate organ function, and reasonable performance status. A free case review determines eligibility.

### Which hospitals in China offer CAR-T for lymphoma?
**Ruijin Hospital (Shanghai)**, **Peking University Cancer Hospital (Beijing)**, **Jiahui International Hospital (Shanghai)**, and **Tongji Hospital (Wuhan)** are the top choices for international lymphoma patients. All have international departments with English-speaking coordinators and visa support.

### How long does CAR-T treatment for lymphoma take in China?
**4-6 weeks in China**: apheresis (1 day) → manufacturing (2-4 weeks) → conditioning chemo (3-5 days) → infusion (1 day) → monitoring (10-14 days). Add 2-4 weeks before for visa and travel.

### Is CAR-T for lymphoma safe in China?
At JCI-accredited or NMPA-certified centers, outcomes are comparable to international standards. Commercial products are NMPA-approved; trial products follow ICH-GCP guidelines. International departments provide English-speaking care, CRS management, and ICU monitoring on-site.
