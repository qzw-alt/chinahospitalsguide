## Quick Answer: Knee Replacement in China, 2026

**Total knee replacement in China costs $8,000-$12,000** — about 70% below the $35,000-$50,000 typical in the US, and roughly half the $16,000-$24,000 price in Singapore. Surgery is available **1-2 weeks after arrival**, compared with 12-18 month NHS waits in the UK. The best hospitals — Peking Union Medical College Hospital, Shanghai Ruijin, Guangzhou Zhujiang, and Shenzhen People's Hospital — are top-tier (Grade A, Class 3) public hospitals with international departments, English-speaking coordinators, and surgeons who perform 300-500+ knee replacements per year. The full journey, from records review to flying home, takes about 3-4 weeks including recovery time in China.

## 1. How Much Does Knee Replacement Cost in China? (2026)

| Procedure | China (USD) | US (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Total Knee Replacement (TKR)** | $8,000 – $12,000 | $35,000 – $50,000 | Full joint replacement; most common for severe arthritis |
| **Partial Knee Replacement (PKR)** | $5,000 – $7,000 | $20,000 – $30,000 | Only the damaged compartment is replaced |
| **Knee Resurfacing** | $6,000 – $9,000 | $25,000 – $35,000 | Preserves more bone; suited to younger active patients |

### China vs Singapore vs UK vs US

| Country | Total Knee Replacement (USD) | Typical Wait | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **China** | $8K – $12K | 1-2 weeks | Top-tier 3A public hospitals; same international implant brands (Zimmer, Stryker, DePuy) |
| **Singapore** | $16K – $24K | 2-4 weeks | Excellent private hospitals, higher cost |
| **UK (private)** | $25K – $35K | 2-4 weeks | Private care; NHS wait is 12-18 months |
| **USA** | $35K – $50K | 2-4 weeks | Highest cost; insurance-dependent |

> 2026 estimates based on published data. Why is China cheaper? Lower operating costs, government subsidies to public hospitals, high surgical volume (thousands of joint replacements monthly), and competitive pricing among international departments. Implants are the same international brands used in the US and Europe.

### What's Included vs Additional Costs

**Typical package includes:** pre-surgery consultations and assessment, surgery and anesthesia, hospital stay (7-14 days), all in-hospital medications, physical therapy guidance and rehab plan, English-speaking coordinator, airport pickup, and post-surgery follow-up consultation.

**Budget additionally for:** international flights ($800-$2,000), medical visa (S2, $140-$200), out-of-hospital accommodation if needed ($50-$150/night), meals ($20-$50/day), and home-country follow-up.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does knee replacement surgery cost in China in 2026?
Total knee replacement costs **$8,000-$12,000**, partial $5,000-$7,000, and resurfacing $6,000-$9,000. Compare with $35,000-$50,000 in the US and $16,000-$24,000 in Singapore. (2026 estimates based on published data; final cost depends on hospital and implant choice.)

### How long is the wait for knee replacement in China?
Most patients get surgery **within 1-2 weeks of arrival** after a 24-48 hour records review. This is dramatically faster than the 12-18 month NHS wait in the UK.

### Is the quality of knee replacement in China good?
Yes. Top hospitals are Grade A Class 3 (the highest tier) and many hold JCI or equivalent accreditation. Surgeons perform 300-500+ knee replacements per year, and hospitals use the same international implant brands (Zimmer, Stryker, DePuy, Smith & Nephew) as US hospitals. Surgical site infection rates are comparable to Western centers (approximately 1-2%).

### How do I choose between hospitals?
The right choice depends on your case: complex or revision cases suit PUMCH or Zhujiang; minimally invasive and faster recovery favors Ruijin; lowest cost with modern robotics points to Shenzhen. A free case review matches your condition, budget, and travel route to 2-3 suitable hospitals with written quotes.

### What about post-surgery care after I return home?
Hospitals provide physical therapy guidance, a rehab plan for your home doctor, medication documentation, and follow-up tele-consultations. You should confirm that a home-country doctor can manage post-operative care, and consider medical travel insurance that covers surgical complications.
