## Quick Answer: CAR-T for Multiple Myeloma in China, 2026

**BCMA CAR-T for relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma in China costs $89,000-$151,000 (commercial) or $30,000-$80,000 (clinical trial)** — 65-85% below the two US BCMA products, which list at $437,000-$465,000. China has more NMPA-approved CAR-T products than any other country and is the home of **Legend Biotech**, the Nanjing company that co-developed Carvykti — the world's leading BCMA CAR-T. Eligible patients are usually treated with **no waitlist**, with most slots bookable 2-4 weeks after eligibility is confirmed. The full journey, from records review to infusion, takes about 4-6 weeks in China.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does CAR-T therapy for multiple myeloma cost in China in 2026?
Commercial BCMA CAR-T costs **$89,000-$151,000** in China; clinical trial access can reduce this to **$30,000-$80,000**. The two FDA-approved BCMA products in the US list at **$437,000-$465,000** (Abecma and Carvykti). (2026 estimates based on published data; final cost depends on product, hospital, and case complexity.)

### Which BCMA CAR-T products are approved in China?
China has approved **equecabtagene autoleucel (Fucaso)** and **zevor-cel** for relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma. China is also home to Nanjing-based Legend Biotech, co-developer of Carvykti — the world's leading BCMA CAR-T sold globally.

### Who is eligible for BCMA CAR-T for multiple myeloma in China?
Most programs target **relapsed or refractory myeloma** after at least three prior lines, typically including a proteasome inhibitor and an immunomodulatory drug. Requirements include BCMA expression, adequate organ function, and reasonable performance status. A free case review determines eligibility.

### Which hospitals in China offer BCMA CAR-T for multiple myeloma?
**Ruijin Hospital (Shanghai)**, **Lu Daopei Hospital (Beijing)**, **Fudan University Shanghai Cancer Center**, and **Tongji Hospital (Wuhan)** are the top choices for international myeloma patients. All have international departments with English-speaking coordinators and visa support.

### How long does CAR-T for multiple myeloma take, and is it safe?
**4-6 weeks in China**: apheresis (1 day) → manufacturing (2-4 weeks) → conditioning chemo (3-5 days) → infusion (1 day) → monitoring (10-14 days). Commercial products are NMPA-approved, and top centers manage CRS and neurotoxicity per international protocols with ICU support.
