## Quick Answer: CAR-T Therapy Hospitals in China, 2026

**If you need CAR-T therapy, expect $89,000-$151,000 for a commercial NMPA-approved product in China** — roughly 65-85% below US pricing ($500K-$699K) and 40-60% below Singapore ($280K-$350K). Eligible patients can reduce costs further through clinical trials ($30K-$80K). The five hospitals international patients choose most are **PUMCH (Beijing) for complex hematologic cases, Fudan Cancer Hospital (Shanghai) for the highest CAR-T volume in Asia, Ruijin Hospital (Shanghai) for hematology leadership and trial access, Lu Daopei Hospital (Beijing) for blood-cancer specialization including multiple myeloma, and Peking University People's Hospital (Beijing) for aggressive lymphoma**. All five have English-speaking international departments, accept remote case review, and issue visa invitation letters. The full journey — from records review to infusion — takes about 4-6 weeks in China, with most slots bookable 2-4 weeks after eligibility is confirmed.

## 1. CAR-T Therapy Cost in China vs Singapore, Thailand & US (2026)

CAR-T pricing in China has **two tiers**, which is why quotes for the same therapy can differ by 3-5x between patients.

| Access Route | 2026 Price (USD) | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial (NMPA-approved product)** | $89K – $151K | Patients who want a specific approved product with no trial eligibility requirements |
| **Clinical trial / sponsored access** | $30K – $80K | Eligible patients willing to enroll in a hospital- or manufacturer-sponsored trial |

### Country Comparison (CD19 / BCMA CAR-T, Hematologic Cancers)

| Country | Commercial Price (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **China** | $89K – $151K | 7 NMPA-approved CAR-T products — the most of any country; satri-cel (solid tumor) also approved June 2026 |
| **Singapore** | $280K – $350K | Kymriah available; no domestic CAR-T manufacturing |
| **Thailand** | Not commercially approved | Trial access only; limited slots |
| **United States** | $500K – $699K | 6 FDA-approved products; highest list prices globally |
| **Germany / EU** | $320K – $400K | Approved products available; prices vary by country |

> 2026 estimates based on published data and patient-reported quotes. Travel, accommodation, and bridging chemotherapy are not included. Final cost depends on your specific case — always request a written itemized quote from the hospital's international department.

**What's included in a typical quote:** T-cell collection (apheresis), cell manufacturing, conditioning chemotherapy, the CAR-T infusion, 2-3 weeks of inpatient monitoring, and English-language discharge records. Most hospitals ask for a 30-50% deposit to lock the manufacturing slot.

## 3. Treatment Timeline: From First Contact to Infusion

| Phase | What Happens | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Records review** | Send pathology report, treatment history, PET-CT/biopsy results; hospital confirms CAR-T candidacy | 1-3 days |
| **2. Treatment plan & quote** | Written plan: product, cost estimate, trial options if eligible | 3-10 days |
| **3. Visa & booking** | Hospital issues medical visa invitation letter (M visa); book flights to Beijing/Shanghai/Guangzhou | 1-2 weeks |
| **4. T-cell collection** | Leukapheresis — blood drawn, T-cells separated, rest returned; no general anesthesia | 1-2 days |
| **5. Cell processing** | Cells engineered in GMP lab; bridging chemo may be given to control disease | 10-14 days |
| **6. Conditioning + infusion** | Short lymphodepleting chemo (3-5 days), then CAR-T infusion via IV (30 minutes) | 4-6 days |
| **7. Monitoring & discharge** | Inpatient monitoring for CRS/neurotoxicity management; discharge with follow-up schedule | 2-3 weeks |
| **8. Remote follow-up** | Blood tests and PET-CT at home hospital; results shared with Chinese team via telemedicine | Ongoing |

**Total time in China: 4-6 weeks** for most CAR-T patients. Plan accordingly.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Which hospitals in China offer CAR-T therapy to international patients?
**PUMCH, Fudan Cancer Hospital, Ruijin Hospital, Lu Daopei Hospital, and Peking University People's Hospital** — all with dedicated international departments, English-speaking staff, and visa support. For private Western-style care, SinoUnited Hospital (Shanghai) also runs a dedicated international CAR-T center at $130K-$165K.

### How much does CAR-T therapy cost in China in 2026?
**$89,000-$151,000 for commercial NMPA-approved products**, or **$30,000-$80,000 via clinical trials** for eligible patients. That's 65-85% below US pricing and roughly 40-60% below Singapore. Final cost depends on product, hospital, and whether bridging chemotherapy is needed.

### How long does CAR-T treatment take in China?
**4-6 weeks on-site** — apheresis (1-2 days), cell processing (10-14 days), conditioning chemo (3-5 days), infusion, then 2-3 weeks of monitoring. Slots are typically bookable 2-4 weeks after eligibility is confirmed.

### Is CAR-T therapy in China safe?
Commercial products are **NMPA-approved**, and top centers manage CRS and neurotoxicity per international protocols with ICU support. JCI-accredited or NMPA-certified hospitals report outcomes comparable to international benchmarks for eligible patients.

### Can international patients join CAR-T clinical trials in China?
**Yes**, especially for solid tumors (liver, gastric, pancreatic) and autoimmune diseases. Eligibility is strict, screening is not guaranteed, and outcomes are uncertain — but trial participation may reduce or eliminate product costs.
